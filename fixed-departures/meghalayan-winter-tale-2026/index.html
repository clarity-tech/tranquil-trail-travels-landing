A Meghalayan Winter Tale

Seven days through the Khasi and Jaintia Hills, where the post-monsoon light turns the valleys to mist and gold.

Meghalaya, the abode of clouds, reveals itself in winter as a pristine wonderland of endless scenic views, sweeping valleys, and dramatic gorges. The post-monsoon charm leaves the rolling hills bathed in mist and gold — the perfect canvas for your own story.

This seven-day fixed departure traverses the heart of the Khasi and Jaintia Hills. From tracing historic trekking trails to sharing meals with communities in secluded villages, every moment is crafted to leave you with memories of a lifetime.

Northeast India is not a destination. It is an invitation.

Route: Guwahati → Shillong → Sohra → Pongtung → Shnongpdeng → Shillong → Guwahati

Please ensure your flight lands at Guwahati Airport by or before 11:00 AM on Day 1.

What This Journey Holds

Discover the mystical caves, dramatic viewpoints, and hidden gems of Sohra.

Day by Day

Seven Days in the Hills

Day 1 · Guwahati to Shillong

Pineapples & Lakes

Your journey commences the moment you land. Our trip leader welcomes you at Guwahati Airport, and we step into the Urbania to ascend into the hills on a scenic drive toward Shillong. En route we stop at Nongpoh for the country's juiciest pineapples, followed by a breathtaking halt at the expansive Umiam Lake. Spend your inaugural evening absorbing the unique energy of Shillong — wander its colourful street markets, discover a cosy local café, or simply unwind at your stay.

Shillong

Day 2 · Shillong to Sohra

Footsteps in Time

After a hearty breakfast we step back in time along the David Scott Trail, a historic route winding through emerald valleys, rolling hills, and archaic stone bridges. An expert local guide leads the way, sharing the legends woven into the landscape, and lunch is served on the trail. As the afternoon sun dips we drive on into the dramatic terrain of Sohra (Cherrapunji). Settle in, unwind, and as dusk falls gather around a crackling bonfire — weather and local regulations permitting — to share stories under a starlit sky.

SohraBreakfast, Lunch

Day 3 · Sohra sightseeing

Mystical Caves & Scenic Vistas

Awaken to the misty embrace of Sohra. Following breakfast we dedicate the day to the region's geographical treasures — ancient caves, and dramatic viewpoints offering uninterrupted views across the Meghalayan gorges, all with a local guide. After a satisfying local lunch we spend the afternoon among the scenic landscapes and perennial waterfalls hidden deep within the forests. Every stop offers a picture-perfect moment and a chance to meet the warm Khasi locals.

SohraBreakfast, Lunch

Day 4 · Sohra to Pongtung

Culture & Communities

The journey draws deeper into the authentic rural charm of Meghalaya. Leaving Sohra for Pongtung, our local guide introduces the magnificent Mawkhynot Root Bridge — a serene gem well away from the well-trodden paths. After a local lunch we move from wild forest to the immaculate lanes of Mawlynnong, known as Asia's cleanest village, where you'll relish authentic local food and observe the quiet harmony of village life in the Khasi hills.

PongtungBreakfast, Lunch

Day 5 · Pongtung to Shnongpdeng

The Glass River

A day of pure aquatic magic. We make our way to Shnongpdeng, renowned for the crystalline waters of the Umngot River, where boats appear to hover mid-air. A full day of river activities is included — kayak across the transparent water, leap from the cliffs into the cool depths, or simply bask on the pebbled shores. After lunch by the water we camp in tents right by the riverside, falling asleep to the lullaby of the river, warmed by a bonfire under a canopy of stars.

Shnongpdeng (riverside tents)Breakfast, Lunch

Day 6 · Shnongpdeng to Shillong

Vistas and Valleys

Waking in a tent by the whispering river is an unforgettable thing. After breakfast we bid the waters adieu and begin the scenic ascent back to Shillong, soaking in panoramic views of the hills. En route we detour to the vivid blue of Krang Suri and the hidden majesty of Wahweh. By evening we arrive back in the bustling heart of Shillong — the final night is yours to celebrate the journey, hunt for souvenirs, and savour a farewell dinner in the city.

ShillongBreakfast
Carrying the Mist Home

Day 7 · Shillong to Guwahati

Carrying the Mist Home

Every beautiful chapter must eventually close. After breakfast, with hearts full of mist and gold, we bid goodbye to the scenic hills of Meghalaya. Our trip leader escorts the group in the Urbania for the final descent to Guwahati Airport. You carry home not just photographs, but stories, renewed spirits, and the warm bonds of new friendships.

FAQ

Before You Book

What time should my flight land in Guwahati?

Please ensure your flight lands at Guwahati Airport by or before 11:00 AM on Day 1 (25th December). The group departs together for Shillong from the airport, and later arrivals will miss the Nongpoh and Umiam Lake stops.

Is this a private trip or a shared group departure?

It is a shared fixed departure with a maximum of 11 travellers, designed so solo travellers and small groups meet each other. To keep the group balanced we allow a maximum of 2 people booking together who already know each other. If you would prefer a private version of this itinerary, message us and we will craft one for your dates.

What is the accommodation like?

Accommodation is on a twin-sharing basis — homestays and resorts in the towns, and tents at Shnongpdeng on Day 5. The riverside camping night is a highlight of the trip rather than a compromise, with a bonfire and all river activities included.

How cold does Meghalaya get in late December?

Winter in the Khasi and Jaintia Hills is crisp and dry rather than harsh — days are mild and clear, and nights in Sohra and Shillong drop close to single digits. Pack layers, a warm jacket for the evenings and the riverside camp, and shoes with grip for the David Scott Trail.

How fit do I need to be for the David Scott Trail?

The trail is a moderate day walk along a historic route rather than a technical trek. A reasonable level of everyday fitness is enough. The stated age group for this departure is 15 to 55 years, and a local expert guides the group throughout with lunch provided on the trail.

What is not included in the ₹28,800?

Flights and trains to Guwahati, lunches on Day 1, Day 6 and Day 7, dinners on all days, personal expenses and tips, and any costs from unforeseen weather or roadblocks. Everything else — transport, stays, the trip leader, local guides on Days 2 to 5, and all Shnongpdeng activities — is covered.
